# NNNN. Title

**Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
**Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by [ADR-00NN](00NN-title.md)

## Context

What is the issue we're facing? What forces (technical, legal, organizational) are at play? State facts, not opinions.

## Decision

What did we decide to do? State it plainly, in one or two sentences.

## Consequences

What becomes easier or harder as a result? What did we give up? What follow-up work does this create?

**ステータス:** Proposed | Accepted | Deprecated | Superseded by [ADR-00NN](00NN-title.md)

## 背景

直面している課題は何か。どんな力(技術的、法的、組織的)が働いているか。意見ではなく事実を書く。

## 決定

何をすると決めたか。1文か2文で率直に書く。

## 結果

その結果、何が容易になり、何が難しくなるか。何を諦めたか。どんな後続の作業が生まれるか。
